# Policy & Decision System (PDS)

> **The decision execution core of DBM Structural Intelligence**

---

## 1. What Is PDS?

The **Policy & Decision System (PDS)** is the component in DBM-SI responsible for transforming structural signals into executable decisions.

At its core, PDS performs:

    Policy → Scoring → Strategy → Decision → Execution

But in the DBM-SI framework, it extends far beyond that.

## 2. What Makes PDS Unique

Unlike traditional decision engines, PDS explicitly models:

- **branching decisions** (promoted / shadow / fallback)
- **multi-round trajectory behavior**
- **state-conditioned memory**
- **policy-family competition**
- **comparative validation**
- **evidence and artifact generation**

This turns decision-making into a **structured and inspectable process**, not just a single output.

## 3. Role in DBM-SI Architecture

    Structural Signals (CCC)
            ↓
    Context Localization
            ↓
    PDS (Decision Core)
            ↓
    Trajectory Formation
            ↓
    Memory & Evolution
            ↓

PDS is the **bridge between structure and action**.
