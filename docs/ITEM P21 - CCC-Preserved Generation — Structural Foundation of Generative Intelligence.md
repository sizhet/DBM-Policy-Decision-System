# CCC-Preserved Generation — Structural Foundation of Generative Intelligence

## 1. Abstract

This work introduces **CCC-Preserved Generation (CPG)** as the structural foundation of generative intelligence within the DBM-SI paradigm.

We argue that true generative intelligence is not characterized by probabilistic output fluency, but by the **preservation and controlled evolution of structural invariants**, 
defined as Common Concept Cores (CCC).

CPG reframes generative systems as **structure-constrained transformation operators**, enabling stable accumulation of intelligence under **Minimal Evolution Threshold (MET)** constraints.

Modern generative models (e.g., LLMs) operate primarily as:

However, they lack:

- controlled structural evolution

This leads to:

- inconsistency across generations

- limited accumulation of intelligence
