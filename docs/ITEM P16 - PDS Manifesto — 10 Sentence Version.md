# ITEM P16
# PDS Manifesto — 10 Sentence Version

（PDS 十句宣言）

## English Version
1. Intelligence is not prediction — it is decision under constraint.
2. The future is not singular — it is a space of possibilities.
3. True intelligence selects among futures, rather than predicting one.
4. Every decision is shaped by an explicit or implicit policy.
5. Policy defines what “good” means, not just what is possible.
6. Without policy, intelligence is uncontrolled generation.
7. Knowledge defines space, trajectory defines motion, policy defines selection.
8. Learning is not only improving decisions, but evolving policy.
9. Multi-agent systems transform individual decisions into world dynamics.
10. The future of AI is not larger models, but controllable decision systems.

## 中文点睛版（建议放 README / 海报）
1. 智能不是预测，而是在约束下的选择。
2. 未来不是一个点，而是一片空间。
3. 真正的智能，是在多未来中做选择。
4. 每个决策，都受策略支配。
5. 策略定义“好”，而不只是“可行”。
6. 没有策略，只有失控的生成。
7. 知识定空间，轨迹定演化，策略定选择。
8. 学习不只是优化决策，而是演化策略。
9. 多智能体，让决策变成世界动力学。
10 AI 的未来，不是更大模型，而是可控决策系统。

## 🔥 One-Line Manifesto（压缩版）

> **AI is not about predicting the future —**\
> **it is about deciding which future to realize.**
