## 2. Diagram Interpretation

### 🔷 Bidirectional Structure

PDS is not a unidirectional pipeline, but rather a dual-stream structure：

### ▶ Generative Flow
    Knowledge → State → Candidates → Decision → Policy

- Generating Possibilities Based on Capabilities.
- Building Future Spaces.

### ▶ Control Flow
    Policy → Decision → Candidates → State → Knowledge

- Constrain choices based on objectives.
- Reverse Shaping System Behavior.

### 🔥 Key Conclusions

> **Intelligence = A Coupled System of Generative Flow × Control Flow**

## 3. Fish-Control Interpretation

This structure corresponds to：

- Fish (Behavior) move within the current (Policy).
- and continuously adjust through the internal system (Decision).

#### Relations：

|Layer	|Biological Analogy
|---|---|
|V Policy	|Flow / Potential Field
|IV Decision	|Nervous System
|III Candidates	|Actionable Steps
|II State	|Perception
|I Knowledge	|Physiological Structure

#### 🔥 Nature

> **Control does not act directly upon the outcome, but rather upon the "shape of the possibility space."**

## 4. Canonical Caption

> **Figure P02 — PDS Canonical Diagram.**\
> The Policy Decision System is structured as a bidirectional control architecture composed of five interacting layers: Knowledge, State, Candidate Space, Decision Engine, and Policy.

> The generative flow constructs possible futures, while the control flow shapes selection through policy constraints.

> Intelligence emerges from the coupling of these two flows, forming a Fish-Control Structure where behavior is guided rather than explicitly commanded.
